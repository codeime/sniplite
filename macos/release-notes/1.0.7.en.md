- Fixed: leaving a capture with drawings asked a question that could open hidden under the capture screen of another display, so sniplite seemed frozen and nothing on the Mac could be clicked. The question is gone: Esc, ✕ and right-click leave at once. And if sniplite ever stops responding during a capture, it quits by itself after a few seconds and opens again, so the screen is never locked.
- Open menus can be captured: they stay in the picture, and the selection snaps to the menu itself.
- A new arrow: one shape with an even shaft, a round end and a swept-back head. See-through drawings no longer show darker where their parts overlap, and color and opacity changes show while you drag.
- The toolbar is frosted glass: what is under it shows through, blurred.
- The menu bar menu shows the shortcuts in the system's own column; "Permissions…" appears only while one is missing, and says which.
- Allowing a permission with System Settings on another desktop: the guide follows it there, and you come back to your desktop afterwards.
