- With more than one display: the magnifier shows only on the display with the mouse (it no longer sits in the top-left corner of the others), and the display the mouse left goes dim instead of lighting up whole. A click, a key or the wheel right after moving to another display acts on what is under the mouse there.
- The cursor no longer flickers while you choose an area. It is a small cross with a white edge, easy to see on dark screens too.
- The magnifier has a dark edge and a soft shadow, so it no longer blends into white pages.
