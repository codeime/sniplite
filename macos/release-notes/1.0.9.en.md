- Text recognition is ready much sooner: the first one took about half a minute, now a few seconds (Chinese, English and other languages of Apple's earlier model; Japanese and Korean are as before). A notice shows while it reads, and says when the text is copied, when there is none, or when it failed.
- One kind of notice for everything: quick capture, copying an image, a color or text, text recognition. It follows the look of macOS (Liquid Glass from macOS 26 on), shows where you choose in Settings › General, and is never in the next capture.
- Pressing C to copy a color now says so; the message used to be hidden under the capture screen.
- The theme (as macOS, light or dark) is for all of sniplite, no longer for the capture toolbar alone.
- Settings: Quick capture is now Capture, with text recognition and pins as well.
- The toolbar's icons are centered and their lines more alike; the icon for copying text is a new one.
- On macOS 26 and later, sniplite has the current look of switches, buttons and windows.
